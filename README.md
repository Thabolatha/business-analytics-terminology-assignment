# Business Analytics Terminology Assignment

## Overview

This repository contains my handwritten and scanned PDF submission for the Business Analytics — Foundations assignment titled The Language of Business Analytics.

The assignment focuses on researching, defining, and applying 38 essential business analytics terms that explain how modern businesses use data to make decisions.

## Purpose of the Assignment

The purpose of this assignment is to build a strong understanding of the core vocabulary used in business analytics, data reporting, customer performance measurement, and marketing campaign analysis.

This assignment helped me understand how businesses use data to measure performance, make better decisions, understand customers, and evaluate marketing campaigns.

## Assignment Sections

The assignment is divided into four main sections:

1. Foundational Concepts of Analytics  
2. Data, Measurement & Reporting  
3. Customer & Sales KPIs  
4. Marketing & Campaign Analytics  

## Files Included

- `Business_Analytics_Assignment.pdf` — Scanned handwritten version of the completed assignment
- `README.md` — Overview and purpose of the assignment

## Learning Outcome

This assignment demonstrates my understanding of key business analytics terms, KPIs, and reporting concepts. It also shows how analytics supports decision-making, customer management, sales performance, and marketing evaluation.
